Software & Tools

Open-source computational frameworks and analytical tools developed for understanding spatial omics, multiscale interactions, and social neuroscience.

SpiderNet

Infers directional cell–cell meta-interactions from spatial transcriptomics using interpretable deep learning integrating graph neural networks, non-negative factorization, and attention mechanisms.

[Tang et al., Nature Cell Biology (in revision)]

SpaHDmap

Performs interpretable high-resolution dimensionality reduction for spatial transcriptomics, aligning biological activity patterns with spatial context for fine-grained visualization and interpretation.

[Tang et al., bioRxiv]

CellFlowMap

Models dynamic cell–cell communication flow and perturbation effects across time points, extending SpiderNet to infer temporal meta-interaction networks.

[Tang et al., in preparation]

SOAR-X

Self-supervised attention model that decomposes cell interactions into intrinsic, local, and global components, providing biologically interpretable embeddings.

[Tang et al., bioRxiv]

Eykthyr

Infers region-specific transcription factor influences on spatial gene programs (metagenes) using spatial multiome data.

[Krieger, Haber, and Ma, bioRxiv]
