About / Research

My research integrates computational neuroscience and spatial omics to understand how biological systems organize and communicate across scales.

Interpretable Models for Spatial Omics & Tissue Organization

We develop interpretable graph-based models to infer directional cell–cell communication networks and identify multiscale spatial modules that shape tissue structure and function.

Computational Modeling of Social Decision-Making

We investigate how people learn and make social decisions by integrating reinforcement learning, social preference modeling, and neuroimaging (fMRI). Our models aim to explain individual differences in prosocial behavior and cognitive flexibility.

Graph Neural Networks for Multiscale Biological Interactions

We employ graph neural networks (GNNs) and attention-based architectures to capture hierarchical patterns of biological interactions, from single-cell communication to tissue-level coordination.
