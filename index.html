About Me

I’m a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Computational Biology Department (School of Computer Science) at Carnegie Mellon University, working with Prof. Jian Ma.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Peking University (Center for Statistical Science; School of Mathematical Sciences), advised by Prof. Ruibin Xi, and my B.S. in Statistics from South China Normal University.

My research focuses on developing statistical inference models and interpretable machine-learning frameworks to address problems in computational biology.
I am particularly interested in cell–cell communication in spatial omics and multiscale modeling / foundation models for tissue organization.

Outside research, I enjoy photography, cooking, traveling, and exploring new cultures.
