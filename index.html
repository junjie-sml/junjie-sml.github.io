“Science is the art of making sense of complexity.”

Hello! I am Junjie Tang (唐俊杰). I am a postdoctoral researcher at Carnegie Mellon University. My research focuses on developing interpretable computational models to understand how intercellular communication organizes tissue structure and how neural systems support social decision-making in the human brain.

I received my PhD in Computational Biology and Neuroscience, and my work bridges machine learning, spatial transcriptomics, and social neuroscience.

Outside research, I enjoy photography, cooking, traveling, and exploring new cultures. Feel free to reach out if you’d like to connect!
